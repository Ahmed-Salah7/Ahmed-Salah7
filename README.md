## 🧭 Leadership principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Clarity > Activity** | Crisp goals, clear ownership, realistic timelines |
| **Quality by default** | Reviews, testing, CI, and observability as habits, not afterthoughts |
| **People grow, products win** | Regular 1:1s, honest feedback, deliberate skill development |
| **Outcomes over outputs** | Align work to customer impact and measurable signals |

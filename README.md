### 👋 About me
I’m a **Team Lead Software Engineer** who builds reliable products and strong teams. I guide delivery, mentor engineers, design pragmatic solutions, and remove blockers so we can ship value consistently.

- **What I do:** lead delivery, architecture & reviews, mentoring, and process improvement.  
- **How I work:** clarity first, iterate fast, automate the boring parts, measure what matters.

---

### 🧭 Leadership principles
- **Clarity > Activity:** crisp goals, ownership, and timelines.  
- **Quality by default:** reviews, testing, CI, and observability as habits.  
- **People grow, products win:** regular 1:1s, feedback, and skill development.  
- **Outcomes over outputs:** align work to customer impact and measurable signals.
